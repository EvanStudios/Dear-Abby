▶ Her
Taken on August 9, 2026

Dear Abby,

It has officially been twelve months since I first met you. When you walked in my first thought was, “Wow, she’s so beautiful”. My second thought was, “I’m sure this won’t last long, but I’ll enjoy the time we have”. I was immediately swept up by your smile, the passion you spoke with, and the brightness you brought with you. The calls we had were amazing, but meeting you made it abundantly clear that I needed more of Abby Lammers.

I have always had some trouble reading you, but gradually you have let me in to see who you are, and how your mind works. You might just be the most brilliant person I have ever met (please don’t bring this up in an argument for an easy win). We think so differently, and I believe we complement each other in so many ways. You help me see new perspectives and challenge me to gain a deeper understanding of the world, and what it means to be a child of God. You engage me in ways nobody else ever has— from the moments we spend in each other’s arms, to staying up late in deep conversation. When I'm with you, I feel at home. When I get a glimpse inside your mind, I remember how lucky I am.

I have had the privilege of getting to know someone who is pure of heart, generous in love, and selfless in spirit. I consider it an honor to be among those who get to see the real you. You have tremendous strength in character, tenacity in pursuing success, and a determination in being a light for everyone around you. I always want to be the best version of myself, but when we’re together, I want to be that person for you more than anything. You deserve every moment of joy, every bit of success, and every ounce of love that comes your way. Even one year in, I still am not sure if you are real.

I talked before about being ready to face life’s challenges with you. You and I have now had very real, and sometimes challenging topics to discuss. We have discovered more about what healthy communication means to us both, and the value of understanding and grace. The way we treat each other in moments where we don’t see eye to eye will continue to be paramount in our journey. I will always hear you, always respect you, and always put your needs above my own. I promise to always do everything in my power to make you happy, to keep you safe, and to never take you for granted.

These past twelve months have given me deeper feelings than I thought possible. In searching for my person, I started to feel like nobody could match the image I created in my mind. The woman I was looking for was smart, kind, sassy, caring, beautiful, and pure of heart. She was great with kids, positive in spirit, and always had a zest for life. She would help me continue to grow as a person, and one day, as a husband and as a father. These are all amazing qualities to find in a woman… but to my surprise, this is not half of what I actually needed. I cannot find the words to describe what you bring to my life, and it’s more than I could ever have imagined. As the song goes— I didn’t know what I was looking for, until I found you.

She could also make incredible mashed potatoes. I have yet to taste your potatoes, but everything else on my list lines up so well that I can overlook this if they are terrible.

Abby, thank you for an entire year of unimaginable joy, peace, and so much laughter. Thank you for accepting me and all of my oddities. Thank you for showing me how deeply I can love another person. Thank you for being you.

I am thrilled to be renewing my yearly membership with you. I love you sweetheart.

- Love, Evan
